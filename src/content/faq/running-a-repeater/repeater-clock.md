---
question: Why is my repeater's clock wrong, or showing 2024?
order: 20
---
Most repeaters don't have a clock that survives a reboot, so they start over at MeshCore's zero date, May 2024. Log in from your companion and tap Sync Clock after every reboot.

If the clock is set in the future, MeshCore won't let you move it backward, so reboot and sync again. A wrong clock doesn't stop the repeater from relaying, but other radios will ignore its adverts.

---
question: Is there coverage where I live?
order: 10
---
Coverage is uneven, and it changes every month as people put up repeaters. The network reaches roughly from Killeen and Belton in the north to San Antonio in the south, and from Fredericksburg in the west to Bastrop in the east. Central Austin and the areas around the big rooftop sites are strongest. The outer suburbs are patchy, and hills and valleys matter more than distance.

The best test is to try it: send a message in Public from the highest spot you can reach and look for ["Heard N repeats"](#heard-repeats). If nothing reaches you, a repeater on your roof, or on a friend's roof up the hill, is often what connects your area. Post your neighborhood in [Discord](https://discord.gg/6a5Sv2s9bG) and members will tell you what's near you.

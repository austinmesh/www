---
question: Does it cost anything to use the network?
order: 20
---
No. The network is free to use. You only need your own radio to connect to it. See [Devices](/devices/) for hardware we suggest.

Austin Mesh isn't a business or a nonprofit. It's run by hobbyists who pay for nodes, antennas, and hosting out of pocket. Read [how Austin Mesh makes money](/about/#money) for the details, including the affiliate links on this site.

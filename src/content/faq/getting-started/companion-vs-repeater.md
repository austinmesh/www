---
question: What's the difference between a companion and a repeater? Do I need both?
order: 40
---
A companion is the radio you send messages from, paired to your phone or with a built in keyboard. It does not relay other people's messages. A repeater only relays and can't send messages of its own. The firmware you install/flash determines which one a radio is.

Two companions in range can talk to each other directly, but to reach the wider mesh you need to be in range of a repeater.

More in [Choose a role](/join/meshcore-setup/#2-choose-a-role-companion-vs-repeater).

question: Is it private or secure?
order: 40
---
Treat it like a hobby radio, not secure messaging.

- Anyone can read Public.
- Anyone who knows a hashtag channel's name can read that channel, because the key comes from the name.
- Direct messages are encrypted, but the radios that carry them can still see some information about who is talking to whom.
- Locations you share are exact unless you move the pin yourself.

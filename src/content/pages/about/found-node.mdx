# I found a (Meshtastic or MeshCore) node on my property, what do I do?

First: Unless you receive explicit permission from a property or land owner, DO NOT install a radio. Doing so actively puts the Austin Mesh community at risk by damaging relationships and public perception.

If you found a radio on your property or property you are responsible for, feel free to remove it. Look for any identifying information left by the owner and reach out to them directly. If you are unable to contact them, feel free to reach out to us via email at info@austinmesh.org and we will do whatever we can to assist you.

## Austin Water Incident

In September of 2026, a radio was found on Austin Water property resulting in the [following letter](/docs/CityofAustin_AustinMesh_Repeater_Notice_Sept2026.pdf).

<blockquote>
During a recent patrol of the conservation lands that we manage , Austin Water Wildland Conservation Division staff found an unauthorized wireless network repeater on City of Austin property . Staff removed the repeater at that time.
The property is part of the Balcones Canyonlands Preserve , which protects habitat for endangered species as mitigation for development across western Travis County . While we manage public trails nearby , off-trail use is not permitted and negatively impacts the preserve. Please remind the Austin Mesh community:
 - Trespassing and leaving unauthorized items on City of Austin property are prohibited under Texas Penal Code §30.05 and Texas Health and Safety Code § 365.012, respectively.
 - Austin Water staff will continue to monitor the MeshCore Node Map and remove
any unauthorized devices found on City property .
 - If the person who placed this repeater would like to claim it (“E621 24.5db 1050ft” on the MeshCore Node Map), they can call or email Austin Water staff at 512 -972 -1660 or wildlands@austintexas.gov to schedule a time to retrieve it from our office in Bee Cave during business hours.
</blockquote>

question: Which antenna should I get? Are the 10 dBi antennas on Amazon real?
order: 10
---
Don't trust the gain printed on the box. Most small "10 dBi" whips really measure 0–3 dBi. The Austin Mesh community uses [rfindex.com](https://www.rfindex.com/mesh/antennas) to publish antenna tests. Its product links are affiliate links. Common picks:

- **Companions:** a Gizont or Muzi Works 17 cm whip.
- **Repeaters:** the Rokland fiberglass antennas.

Around 6 dBi suits most installs. Higher gain flattens the signal pattern, so you can lose nearby nodes below you. The stubby antennas that ship with most radios are not tuned for the correct frequencies and majorly impact performance.

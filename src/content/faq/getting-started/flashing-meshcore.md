---
question: How do I flash MeshCore? My computer doesn't see my radio.
order: 60
---
Use [flasher.meshcore.io](https://flasher.meshcore.io/) in Chrome or Edge, because they support WebSerial. If your computer doesn't see the radio:

1. Try a different USB cable. Many cables carry power only, and this is the most common problem.
2. Look up specific instructions for your radio/chipset.
   - Some radios require holding BOOT down while plugging the radio in
   - Some radios require a specific key press sequence to enter bootloader mode
   - Some radios may require drivers depending on your operating system

Full steps are in [Flash the firmware](/join/meshcore-setup/#3-flash-the-firmware).

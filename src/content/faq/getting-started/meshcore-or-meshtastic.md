---
question: Should I use MeshCore or Meshtastic in Austin?
order: 30
---
**MeshCore.** Almost all Austin activity is on MeshCore now. The Public channel sees hundreds of messages every day, while Meshtastic is quiet and mostly made of older, unmaintained nodes. Meshtastic can still work for a small group of friends who are close together, but if you want to reach the wider Austin mesh, use MeshCore. Most radios run either firmware, so you can switch later.

See [Choosing a platform](/join/) for a short overview, or the [in-depth comparison](/about/meshcore-vs-meshtastic/).

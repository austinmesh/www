---
question: What does "Heard N repeats" mean? How do I know my message got out?
order: 20
---
It counts how many times your radio directly heard a nearby repeater pass your message on. You cannot tell the total number of repeats on the network, because you cannot directly hear all repeaters on the network.

Zero repeats means that your radio did not hear any repeaters pass your message on. It **does not necessarily** mean your message didn't make it to the network, only that your radio didn't hear itself being repeated.

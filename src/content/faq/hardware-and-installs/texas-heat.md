---
question: Will Texas heat kill my node or its battery?
order: 50
---
The battery is the weak point, not the radio. Shade the enclosure, ideally under the solar panel, use a light-colored box, and add a breather vent. Skip cheap pillow-pack LiPo batteries and opt for protected 18650s or use a Voltaic Systems V25.

With those, members' builds have survived several Austin summers. The Voltaic-powered repeater on our [devices page](/devices/#solar) has run on solar for over 2.5 years. That section also has our lessons learned from Austin heat.

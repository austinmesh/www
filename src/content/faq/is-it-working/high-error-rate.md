---
question: Why is my error rate so high?
order: 40
---
99.9% of the time it's nothing to worry about. An "error" is a packet your radio started to hear but couldn't decode. The more radios yours can hear, the more often two of them transmit at the same moment and "step" on each other, and the more weak/partial packets it catches.

A better antenna or a higher spot **will raise your error rate** while you're also decoding more packets than before. For example, a repeater that hears 100 packets and decodes 50 has a 50% error rate. Put a better antenna on it, and it might hear 300 and decode 100. The rate goes up to 66%, but the repeater handled twice as many packets.

Error rates of 50–90% are common on well-connected repeaters.

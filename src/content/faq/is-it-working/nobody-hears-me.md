---
question: I can hear other people, but nobody hears me. Why?
order: 30
---
If you can see messages, your settings are almost certainly fine. Radio links aren't symmetric. The big repeaters are high up, with good antennas, so you can hear them from places where they can't hear you. Anytime you work with radios, prioritize changes in this order:

1. **Height.** Move the radio higher, near a window, or outside.
2. **Antenna.** Use a real/tested 915 MHz antenna. Bring your antenna to the next meetup if you don't have a tester or don't want to buy one (NanoVNA).
3. **Power.** Increasing power is always the last step. The current world record is 330km using 0.2w. If you aren't being heard, it's unlikely that power will fix it.

Small radios with internal antennas struggle indoors unless you have a repeater on your roof.

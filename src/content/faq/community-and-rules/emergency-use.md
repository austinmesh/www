---
question: Can I rely on it in an emergency?
order: 50
---
It can help, but don't make it your only plan. The hardware and software are still changing, and coverage is uneven. Mesh technology makes a good part of a [PACE plan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PACE_(communication_methodology)).

If emergency use is your goal, get companions for your household, confirm each one connects from home, and consider a repeater on your roof.

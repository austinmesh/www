---
question: How far will my radio reach? Will more power help?
order: 20
---
Height matters most. A handheld at ground level might reach a mile or two. Repeaters high up with good antennas do much better. The longest reliable links on the network are about 20-40 miles.

More power rarely fixes a weak connection unless you already connect sometimes. Long-distance tests at a normal 22 dBm (0.2w) show height is what counts. The world record is 330km with 0.2w.

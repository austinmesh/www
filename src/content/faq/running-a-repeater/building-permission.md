---
question: How do I get permission to put a repeater on a building?
order: 40
---
Start with a relationship. You can provide the [Property Owner FAQ](/about/property-owner-faq-hosting-a-node/) as part of your introduction. Liability is always the first concern, and that page answers it. Most of the sites we've gotten came through someone who already knew the owner or worked there. Offering a small trial install first also helps. Join the Discord server if you want help crafting your approach.

**Never put a node on property without permission.** Unauthorized nodes put the whole community at risk by damaging our relationships with property owners and the public. See [I found a node](/about/found-node/) for what happened when one turned up on City of Austin land.

---
question: Who owns this repeater?
order: 50
---
This is a decentralized network. There is no directory. If the owner left the guest password blank, you can open the repeater in Guest Tools and read its Owner Info (but that info isn't required). You can always ask in [Discord](https://discord.gg/6a5Sv2s9bG), but there's no guarantee of finding the owner.

If you run a repeater, we strongly recommend putting a way to reach you in its Owner Info and writing or printing it physically on the hardware.

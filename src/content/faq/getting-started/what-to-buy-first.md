question: What should I buy first?
order: 50
---
Start with one companion radio that you carry with you. Discord members often recommend the RAK 4631, Heltec T114, Seeed Tracker, and SenseCAP X1.

nRF52 radios last much longer on battery. ESP32 radios such as the Heltec V3 and V4 are cheap but use more power.

A bare board can cost $12–30, but plan on $40–100 once you add a case, a battery, and a good antenna. If a companion works well from home, many people add a repeater up high next.

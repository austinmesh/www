---
question: Can I put my node in the attic?
order: 30
---
Yes, and many members do. The catch is the roof. Radiant barrier and foil-backed decking block a lot of signal, so put the antenna outside or at a vent if you can. Radios handle attic heat fine, but leave the battery out. Always prefer solar power and ensure a battery backup in case the power grid has a hiccup.

If you rent or can't use your roof, the [painter's pole mast](/projects/painters-pole-mast/) project gets a node 30 feet up for under $100.

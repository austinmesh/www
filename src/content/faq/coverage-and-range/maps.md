This is a decentralized network with varying degrees of interconnection to the internet. While there are several maps maintained by community members, it is impossible for any of them to be complete. Radios only appear if their owner chooses to share a location, or if an internet-connected observer heard them.

At this time Austin Mesh does not endorse any specific mapping software, tool, or host. Any maps provided by community members should be treated with care.

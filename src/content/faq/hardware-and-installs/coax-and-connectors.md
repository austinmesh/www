---
question: What coax and connectors should I use? How long can the cable be?
order: 20
---
As short as possible. A 3 dB loss cuts your signal in half, and the wrong cable can lose that much in 10 feet at 910 MHz. Use LMR-240/400 or equivalents. Better still, mount the radio right at the antenna and run USB-C or PoE power up to it.

Outdoors, use an N-type connector, or seal SMA with silicone tape.

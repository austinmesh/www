---
question: I flashed MeshCore and I don't see any contacts. Is something wrong?
order: 10
---
Probably not. Repeaters only announce themselves every 12–48 hours, and companions don't announce on their own, so your contact list will populate slowly over the next few days.

Send a message in Public and look for ["Heard N repeats"](#heard-repeats). If it shows up, you're connected even if your contact list is empty.

If you don't see "Heard N repeats"
1. Check that you picked the **USA/Canada Recommended** preset.
2. Try moving your radio around, even 6 inches can make a difference. Height is might!
